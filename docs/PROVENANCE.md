## Research snapshot

The cutoff is **18 October 2026, 09:22:26 America/Los_Angeles (16:22:26 UTC)**. Model discounting uses the calendar date **18 October 2026**, without a false intraday precision adjustment. Later evidence requires a new documented snapshot.

`data/research.json` contains the 19-entry presentation register with publisher, date, URL, evidence class, intended use and limitation. `data/fact-ledger.json` retains 21 more detailed research facts and their source mappings. `data/presets.json` contains analyst assumptions. Public copies are generated during the build, and the committed result snapshot identifies the model version and valuation date.

### Evidence hierarchy

1. Company announcements establish what the company disclosed about a financing or commercial agreement. They do not establish audited revenue or current cash.
2. Reputable reporting is explicitly attributed, including reports based on unnamed sources or documents not available for independent inspection.
3. Subscription/search extracts are labeled as partial access; they do not imply the underlying company presentation was reviewed.
4. Analyst assumptions are labeled as assumptions and receive no fabricated company citation.
5. Model outputs are calculations, not externally reported facts.

### Important reconciliations

- The Anthropic Series H and OpenAI completed round are post-money equity references. Proposed financing asks and IPO expectations are separate, dated reported discussions.
- Committed funding may include previously announced investments and conditional installments; adding headlines can double count capital. A credit facility limit is not drawn debt.
- Actual-period revenue, annualized revenue and FY forecasts retain their own periods and status. A revenue accounting change requires corresponding margin and capital adjustments.
- The April 2026 OpenAI–Microsoft amendment supersedes older assumptions about reciprocal revenue sharing and exclusivity. Undisclosed percentages and caps are not invented.
- Compute service commitments, leases, investment and funded financial debt require different treatment. The project documents this boundary without pretending to reconstruct unavailable balance sheets.
