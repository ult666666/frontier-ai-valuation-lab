Method and limits

All amounts are present values at 18 October 2026, and the 2026–2036 forecast is unchanged. After 2036 the terminal cash flow grows at the perpetual growth rate and is discounted at WACC. A horizon of N keeps the first N annual cash flows and counts nothing after the cutoff.

EVN = PVforecast + PVterminal × [1 − ((1 + g) / (1 + WACC))N]

A horizon is a stress test, not a forecast of how long a company lasts, a probability or a price. Target values here are operating EV; funding-round prices are equity values.

Method: Damodaran, Valuing Young, Start-up and Growth Companies, pp. 40–41; CFA Institute, FCFF valuation. Implementation, bounds and verification ↗.
