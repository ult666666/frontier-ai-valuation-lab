The financial method

An explicit boundary
on continuation.

All amounts are present values at 18 October 2026. The original operating forecast and its remaining-2026 cash flow are unchanged.

After 2036, the model’s terminal cash flow grows at the selected perpetual growth rate and is discounted at WACC. This lens retains only the first N annual cash flows. It assigns zero cash flow and zero residual value after the cutoff.

EVN = PVforecast + PVterminal × [1 − ((1 + g) / (1 + WACC))N]

This is a conditional stress lens. A selected horizon is not a lifespan forecast, failure probability, security price or investment recommendation. The model uses its existing annual terminal convention; the perpetual reference remains separately visible.

Method: Damodaran, Valuing Young, Start-up and Growth Companies, pp. 40–41; CFA Institute, FCFF valuation. Implementation, bounds and verification ↗.
