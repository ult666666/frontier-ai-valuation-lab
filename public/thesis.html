Read the model’s limits +
  • Forecasts are illustrative analyst assumptions, not probabilities or verified company guidance.
  • Enterprise value measures operating assets. Current net cash, security preferences and a fully diluted ownership bridge remain unverified.
  • Taxes apply only to positive EBIT; no tax-loss carryforwards are modeled. Net reinvestment already includes net capital expenditure and working capital.
  • The terminal period begins after 2036. Changes in its reinvestment policy can create a discontinuity from the final explicit cash flow.
  • Negative EV is preserved. No stock-price floor, investor return, or upside to an equity financing reference is inferred.
