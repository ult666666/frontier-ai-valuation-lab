Method and limits

  • Forecasts are illustrative analyst assumptions, not company guidance or probabilities.
  • The attribution splits a modeled difference across assumption groups. It is not a causal claim.
  • Enterprise value covers operating assets only. Net cash, security preferences and a diluted ownership bridge are not modeled here, and negative values stay negative.
  • Taxes apply only to positive EBIT, with no loss carryforwards. Terminal reinvestment can make the first year after 2036 differ sharply from 2036.
