Why this choice

Illustrative analyst choice: prefer Smaller round because it meets the $25B capital objective and the 88% retained-stake floor with less issuance than More capital. The declared comparison rule is shown separately and must be read alongside the objectives. This is a worked decision, not a real company financing recommendation.

The accepted tradeoff

More capital raises an additional $10B and allocates more signed modeled residual to existing holders when pre-money modeled equity is below the $635B switching point. Smaller round allocates more above that point. New cash is retained; no operating growth benefit or investment return is inferred.

What would change the choice

Revisit the choice if the capital objective rises above $25B, the retained-stake requirement changes, or the quoted terms change. Recalculate the scenario comparison when dated operating evidence or the explicit equity bridge changes; the $635B switching point belongs to these two offers only.

What still needs evidence

Confirm unrestricted pre-close cash, current debt and uncovered claims, participating economic rights, transaction costs, use of proceeds, and the basis of the operating forecasts. Seek evidence of achievable financing terms. The assumed quotes and model-conditioned pricing envelope do not establish capital availability.

07 / Keep the basis next to the conclusion

Method, evidence and limits.

The calculation

Operating value comes from the frozen FCFF engine, with the selected continuation treatment. The common hypothetical bridge gives modeled pre-money residual = operating EV + cash − debt − other claims.

Each round defines its own positive pre-money and post-money prices. Primary proceeds enter retained cash once. Existing-cohort allocation equals its retained fraction multiplied by the modeled post-money residual.

DCF and allocation outputs use floating-point arithmetic. Switching diagnostics derive coefficients from exact entered-term share ledgers, then round the reported result once. Numerical ties and ordinary thresholds use the documented relative band; unresolved results stay unavailable. These conventions do not imply statistical confidence.

The decision boundary

Capital/retention eligibility, model-to-quote coverage and the analyst’s preference are separate. Regret and dominance use eligible submitted plans only, under one stated signed residual metric.

Neither plan counts nor scenario counts imply probabilities. No alternative is claimed to be globally optimal. The analysis excludes preferences, option-pool changes, convertible instruments, fees, future rounds and capital-driven operating benefits.

Analyst notes are inert plain text. Their presence does not certify the conclusion. Current company cap tables, balances and financing availability have not been reconstructed.
