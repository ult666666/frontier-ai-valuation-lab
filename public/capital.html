How this comparison rule works

For each scenario, the benchmark is the largest signed residual allocation among plans meeting both capital and retention objectives. A plan’s gap is that benchmark minus its allocation. The rule returns all plans with the smallest maximum gap across these submitted scenarios. This is minimax regret under the declared metric.

The rule does not change your selection, assign scenario probabilities, or establish investment performance. A missing required value makes the complete rule unavailable; an unfavorable scenario is never discarded.

Calculation warnings and interpretation

    Signed modeled equity residuals and their allocations are not limited-liability share valuations. Negative values remain signed.     The financial interpretation

    A conditional comparison.
    An inspectable method.

    The same operating case is valued before every plan. A hypothetical bridge adds pre-round cash and subtracts debt and other claims. Each primary raise is then added once as retained cash. The existing cohort’s retained fraction allocates the resulting signed model residual.

    Existing cohort allocation = retention × (modeled pre-round equity residual + primary capital)

    Financing quotes are entered independently. The model does not estimate funding availability, investment returns, a company’s true capital need, or the operational benefit of deploying new proceeds. Finite continuation is a truncation assumption; later cash flows and residual value are zero after its cutoff.
