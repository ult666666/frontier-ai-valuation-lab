What matters most

Terminal economics dominate. The present value of the perpetuity accounts for 78.4% of Anthropic Base EV and 99.9% of OpenAI Base EV. Near-term revenue momentum alone cannot settle the valuation question.

Growth consumes capital. The model charges incremental sales for aggregate reinvestment and ties perpetual reinvestment to growth divided by ROIC. High sales growth can destroy value when margins and capital productivity are insufficient.

Financing prices measure something different. Anthropic's completed Series H disclosed $965B post-money equity; OpenAI's closed round disclosed $852B post-money equity. Both remain dated reference points, without an invented current net-cash bridge. [S01] [S02]

Release scope: a shared browser/Node model, a real JSON API, independent Python arithmetic, an editable formula workbook, source provenance and a documented audit. Research and implementation used AI assistance; numerical conclusions are checked through reproducible calculations.

Revenue normalization needs both sides of the ledger

A gross-to-net presentation change can alter reported sales and percentage margins without changing dollar cash flows. A controlled accounting test scales starting revenue and capital turns by the same factor while inversely scaling margins; the model preserves EV. The test is an accounting illustration, not an estimate of either company's partner take rate. [S09]

Evidence that would materially improve the analysis: current unrestricted cash and funded debt; accounting-consistent revenue and operating profit; compute payment and lease schedules; taxable loss details; financing installment conditions; and investor preference or dilution terms. These are model information needs, not assumptions of disclosure.

Inspect the terminal transition

Explicit growth uses a sales-to-capital proxy; perpetual growth uses a separate terminal ROIC. Their difference changes reinvestment at the transition. The implied explicit return is stable margin × (1 − tax) × capital turns; exact next-year reinvestment equivalence also depends on the growth convention. A large jump is an assumption to investigate, not an arithmetic identity to hide.

Treat the deficit as an operating stress measure

The most negative cumulative economic FCFF includes the stub and annual modeled flows. It is not a treasury funding requirement: opening liquidity, monthly cash timing, stock compensation, contractual payment schedules and financing mechanics are not fully modeled. Do not subtract this deficit again from EV.

05 / Calculation contract

One model. Reproducible arithmetic.

Forecast revenue compounds in three growth stages. Margins interpolate from 2027 to 2030, then from 2030 to 2036. Taxes apply only to positive EBIT; there is no loss refund or modeled NOL asset.

Economic FCFF = revenue × economic EBIT margin − modeled tax − Δrevenue / capital turns

The aggregate net reinvestment proxy includes net capital expenditure, capitalized investment and working capital. Adding a second NWC or capex deduction without replacing the proxy would double count investment. Economic margins retain a cash-equivalent allowance for stock compensation.

PV = FCFF / (1 + WACC)^(actual elapsed days / 365.25)

Discount the remaining-2026 input to 31 December 2026 and annual 2027–2036 flows to each year end. This release uses the calendar valuation date 18 October 2026. The 2036 horizon is fixed and visibly labeled.

Terminal FCFF = 2037 NOPAT × (1 − terminal growth / terminal ROIC)

Terminal value = terminal FCFF / (WACC − terminal growth)

Perpetual growth requires capital. The stable-growth relation follows Damodaran's reinvestment/return framework. It does not establish company-specific inputs. Terminal growth must be nonnegative and below both the discount rate and ROIC. [S19]

Reverse DCF without a false monotonicity assumption

Holding other assumptions fixed, EV is a quartic polynomial in 1 + early growth. The solver partitions the 0–200% growth domain at derivative critical points, checks tangent and boundary roots, bisects sign changes and verifies each result through the forward calculation. It returns no solution, one solution or multiple solutions. A future NOL schedule or growth-dependent margin policy would require revisiting this algebra.

Application and API controls

Every required input must be numeric, finite and in range. Unknown keys, blank inputs and invalid terminal relationships are rejected. The Node API limits request bodies to 16 KiB and sensitivity grids to 9 × 9. Browser calculations and the API share the production model; an independent Python implementation and hand-derived fixture check the arithmetic separately.

A stateless backend is sufficient for a deterministic public model. Scenario saving stays in the visitor's browser. No paid API, account setup, database or private investor data is needed to run the project.

06 / Final fortification

Turn failure cases into controls.

The final review preserved the previous terminal and date corrections, then tested custom-input behavior beyond the original preset checks. The resulting fixes are documented and reproducible.

Independent numerical evidence

The V2 baseline passed its existing 7 Python tests and 60 JavaScript assertions. Independent workbook review reconciled 1,164 cached values. These results established consistency, while the newly reproduced failures showed that consistency alone did not validate the input domain.

V3 adds regressions for the demonstrated defects. An independent constant-growth fixture produces $200.314718645B EV. A separate deterministic numerical exercise recovered all 11,203 supported positive forward targets in a 20,000-scenario sample within 1e-6 of their known growth. The Python parity suite compares every forecast field under preset and varied economics.

Release interpretation

This project is ready to demonstrate inspectable financial modeling and full stack engineering within its documented scope. It remains a simplified economic FCFF model with incomplete private-company information. No per-share valuation, probability-weighted target, investment advice or verified current balance sheet is claimed.
